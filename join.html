BEFORE YOU APPLY

Membership criteria

  • Your site publishes primarily original writing.
  • You write regularly about CMMC, NIST SP 800-171, or closely related security work.
  • Your site uses HTTPS and has an RSS or Atom feed.
  • Your most recent substantive post is no more than 180 days old.
  • You disclose sponsored or affiliate material clearly.
  • You agree to install the webring navigation before activation.

Corporate content farms, scraped sites, and fully automated publishing are not accepted. The ring is deliberately human-curated; meeting the checklist does not guarantee inclusion.

APPLICATION

Introduce your blog

Open a membership request on GitHub. The form asks for your site, feed, topics, a short description, and confirmation that you meet the criteria.
